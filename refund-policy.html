Refund Policy

Niger-North College of Health Science and Technology, Kontagora, Niger State — Last updated: July 2026

This Refund Policy applies to school fee payments made through the BRAINS AI platform on behalf of Niger-North College of Health Science and Technology ("the Institution"). Payments are processed by MU'UJIZA DATA as the Institution's technology provider, using Flutterwave and/or Paystack as licensed payment processors.

1. School Fees Are Generally Non-Refundable

Once a school fee payment has been successfully processed and confirmed, it is considered final and non-refundable. This reflects standard practice across Nigerian tertiary institutions, as fees are allocated toward academic resources, staffing, and administration for the semester in question.

2. Exceptions — When a Refund May Apply

A refund or correction may be considered in the following specific circumstances:

  • Duplicate payment: if a technical error causes the same fee to be charged more than once for the same semester.
  • Overpayment: if an amount charged exceeds the officially published fee for your level and semester.
  • Failed but charged transaction: if payment was deducted from your account but never confirmed or reflected in your student portal after a reasonable processing window.

3. How to Request a Correction or Refund

Contact the bursary department directly, providing:

  • Your full name and matric number
  • The payment reference number (shown after checkout, or in your payment confirmation)
  • A brief description of the issue

Requests are reviewed against the Institution's payment records. Verified duplicate or overpayment cases are typically resolved within 5–10 business days, though timing can depend on the response time of the payment processor involved.

4. What We Cannot Refund

We cannot process a refund for a change of mind after a fee has been correctly charged, for fees paid on behalf of a student who later withdraws or transfers, or for any payment where the amount charged matches the Institution's published fee schedule for that level and semester.

5. Processing Time

Approved refunds are returned via the same payment method used for the original transaction. Bank-side processing times are determined by your bank or card issuer and are outside our direct control, but are typically completed within 5–14 business days of approval.

Bursary Contact

For any payment-related concern, contact the bursary office of Niger-North College of Health Science and Technology directly through the student portal, or via the platform's support contact listed on the homepage.
