Privacy Policy

BRAINS AI Academic Intelligence Platform — Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MU'UJIZA DATA ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects personal data through the BRAINS AI platform, on behalf of Niger-North College of Health Science and Technology, Kontagora, Niger State ("the Institution"). This policy is intended to align with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

1. Information We Collect

CategoryExamples
Identity & contact informationFull name, matric number, email address
Academic recordsCourse registrations, exam results, assignment submissions
Payment recordsFee amount, payment status, payment reference, payment date
Technical dataLogin timestamps, IP address (for security and fraud prevention)

We do not collect or store your card number, bank account details, or any sensitive payment credentials. Card and bank information is entered directly into Paystack's or Flutterwave's own secure checkout, which are separately certified payment processors (PCI-DSS compliant). We only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, its amount, and a reference number.

2. Why We Collect This Information

  • To create and manage your student account
  • To process and confirm school fee payments on behalf of the Institution
  • To deliver academic services: exams, assignments, course materials, and live classes
  • To communicate important account or academic notices (e.g. password reset codes, clearance notifications)
  • To detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized account access

3. Who We Share Information With

  • The Institution: academic and payment records are shared with the Institution's administration and bursary staff, since the Platform operates on their behalf.
  • Payment processors (Paystack, Flutterwave): receive only what is necessary to process a transaction. They do not receive your academic records.
  • Email delivery service: used solely to send password reset codes and clearance notifications.

We do not sell personal data to third parties, and we do not use student data for advertising.

4. Data Retention

Academic and payment records are retained for as long as your account is active with the Institution, and afterward for as long as required to meet the Institution's academic record-keeping obligations. You may request deletion of account data that is no longer required to be retained, subject to the Institution's own record-keeping policies.

5. Your Rights

Under NDPR, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data, where retention is not otherwise required
  • Object to certain uses of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact the Institution's administration or the Platform support contact listed on the homepage.

6. Security Measures

We apply technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data involved, including encrypted data transmission, signed and verified payment confirmations, rate limiting against automated abuse, and restricted administrative access on a need-to-know basis.

7. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify the Institution and affected users in accordance with NDPR requirements and applicable timelines.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Data Protection Contact

For any question about how your data is handled, or to exercise your rights under NDPR, contact the Institution's administration or the Platform support contact listed on the homepage.
