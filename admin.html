📋 Catalog Entries

Navigate to this section to load the catalog.

How GPA is calculated: Quality Points (QP) = Grade Point × Credit Units per course. GPA = Total QP ÷ Total Credit Units for the semester. Grade points: A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, F=0. If a course is not in the catalog, it defaults to 3 credit units — students will see a warning on their result slip.
